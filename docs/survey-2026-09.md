# Survey: simplicial formalization in Mathlib, September 2026

Draft of 2026-09-28. What Mathlib has on simplicial sets, model categories and
quasicategories, what is under way upstream, and what that means for the scope of this
repository. All Mathlib claims refer to the revision pinned in `lakefile.toml`
(`110cb7f`, Lean `v4.34.0-rc2`); section 6 says how they were checked and what was not.

## 1. Summary

- **Mathlib has the combinatorial foundations**: the simplex category with all simplicial
  identities; `SSet` with subcomplexes, horns, boundaries, skeleta and nondegenerate simplices;
  products `Δ[m] ⊗ Δ[n]`; nerves and `hoFunctor ⊣ nerve`. It also has Kan complexes,
  quasicategories, inner fibrations, and anodyne and inner anodyne extensions with their
  pushout-products. `Fun(A, X)` (in Lean `(ihom A).obj X`) is a quasicategory, or a Kan complex,
  when `X` is. Simplicial homotopies, `π₀`, and realization ⊣ singular set are there too.
- **Mathlib has a general model category API but no model structure on `SSet`.** The
  Kan–Quillen structure has some of its ingredients in Mathlib (cofibrations, fibrations,
  anodyne extensions, the small object argument), but not the weak equivalences. It is marked "TODO @joelriou" in the
  docstrings. So are "Sing X is Kan" and the Quillen equivalence with spaces.
- **Mathlib lacks the ∞-categorical layer above quasicategories**: left and right fibrations,
  joins and slices, cofinal maps, Joyal's Theorem A, "the spine inclusion is inner anodyne",
  the Joyal model structure and categorical equivalences. `SimplicialHomotopy/` supplies all
  of these except the last two, built on Mathlib's API.
- **Joël Riou is the most active recent author in this part of Mathlib** (section 4.2). His files point to the
  Kan–Quillen model structure and the comparison with spaces. Reedy model structures are a
  parallel line, in the [`reedy`](https://github.com/joelriou/reedy) repository.
- **`Simplicial/` (2024) is covered by Mathlib**, up to one difference: its `KanComplex` also
  requires nonemptiness (section 3.2).

## 2. Proposed scope (not yet adopted)

**In scope: the ∞-categorical layer over Mathlib's `SSet`.**

1. What `SimplicialHomotopy/` has: left and right fibrations and anodyne extensions,
   isofibrations into nerves, conservativity, joins, slices and coslices, cocartesian edges,
   cofinal maps (Lurie, *Higher Topos Theory* (HTT), 4.1.1.1) and Joyal's ∞-categorical version
   of Quillen's Theorem A (HTT 4.1.3.1), and pointwise detection of
   natural equivalences.
2. Next, in this order:
   - the Joyal model structure, as one project: categorical equivalences, isofibrations as a
     class, and "fibrant objects are the quasicategories". The special outer horn theorem and
     the isofibration lemma of the library are first steps.
   - the homotopy coherent nerve of a locally Kan simplicial category is a quasicategory
     (Cordier–Porter, HTT 1.1.5.10). Mathlib has only the definition, and lists this theorem as
     an unattributed project; check upstream before starting.
   - Straightening of left fibrations (HTT 2.2.1) is a Quillen equivalence, so it needs the
     Kan–Quillen structure. Start it only once that is in Mathlib.
3. Stay upstream-compatible: Mathlib naming and namespaces, one notion per concept, and no
   redefinition of anything Mathlib has.

**Out of scope, or used only as a consumer:**

- the Kan–Quillen model structure, "Sing X is Kan" and the Quillen equivalence with spaces.
  These are announced as Riou's TODOs.
- homotopy groups of Kan complexes: not announced, but prepared in Mathlib by Riou's
  `KanComplex/MulStruct`.
- Reedy model structures: the `reedy` repository (section 5).

Where the library needs one of these, it states the fact as a hypothesis or a local lemma, and
switches to Mathlib's version when it lands.

**If adopted, for the existing code:**

- `Equivalence/WeakEquivalence` (weak homotopy equivalences, weak contractibility) would stay as
  an interim notion, because Theorem A needs it, and would not be extended. When Mathlib gets
  weak equivalences of simplicial sets, the first task would be to prove the two notions agree,
  or to switch to Mathlib's.
- The Mathlib pin would be bumped whenever one of the TODOs of section 4.1 lands, and at least
  once a term. After each bump, the greps behind the "not found" rows of section 3.1 would be
  re-run.
- `Simplicial/` would stay frozen as course material, with a note that Mathlib covers it. Its
  `KanComplex` includes `n = 0`; see section 3.2.
- Candidates to offer to Mathlib, smallest first: `Nerve/NerveMap`, `Nerve/NerveGroupoidKan`,
  `Horn/Spine`, then the left and right anodyne extension and fibration files. Discuss them
  first with the maintainers: the Mathlib Zulip, channel `#mathlib4`, or Joël Riou directly.

`SimplicialSet/` in more detail:

- **Basic objects:** `stdSimplex` (`Δ[n]`), `horn` (`Λ[n, i]`), `boundary` (`∂Δ[n]`);
  `Subcomplex`, built on `CategoryTheory/Subfunctor/`; `skeleton` and
  `relativeCellComplexOfMono`; nondegenerate simplices (Eilenberg–Zilber); finite simplicial
  sets; the cartesian closed structure and `unionProd`; `Δ[p] ⊗ Δ[q] ≅` the nerve of
  `Fin (p+1) × Fin (q+1)` (`prodStdSimplex.isoNerve`).
- **Nerves:** `nerve`, `IsStrictSegal (nerve C)`, and `Path` and `spine` (as paths, not as a
  subcomplex). Also `nerveAdjunction : hoFunctor ⊣ nerveFunctor`, `nerveFunctor` fully
  faithful, and `hoFunctor` preserving products.
- **Homotopy:** `SSet.Homotopy f g`, a map `X ⊗ Δ[1] ⟶ Y`; homotopy classes of relative
  morphisms; `π₀`; `KanComplex/MulStruct`, groundwork for `πₙ` with no group yet;
  `sd ⊣ ex`; homology of simplicial sets, with homotopy invariance and a relative long exact
  sequence.
- **Fibrations and anodyne extensions:**
  - `KanComplex S := IsFibrant S` for the scoped fibrations `modelCategoryQuillen.J.rlp`
    (horns `Λ[n + 1, i]`), and `anodyneExtensions := fibrations.llp`, with the small object
    argument.
  - Strong (inner) anodyne extensions, defined by Moss's regular pairings. Moss's
    characterization is not proved yet.
  - Pushout-products: `strongAnodyneExtensions_unionProd_ι` for
    `Λ[m+1, k] ⊗ Δ[n] ∪ Δ[m+1] ⊗ ∂Δ[n]`, `anodyneExtensions_pushoutObjObjι` and
    `innerAnodyneExtensions_unionProd_ι`, with `KanComplex ((ihom A).obj X)` and
    `Quasicategory ((ihom A).obj X)`.

### 3.2 This repository against Mathlib

**`SimplicialHomotopy/` (61 modules)** is built against the pinned Mathlib and uses its
notions: `SSet`, `Subcomplex`, `horn`, `KanComplex`, `Quasicategory`, `innerFibrations`,
`innerAnodyneExtensions`, the pairing API and `unionProd`. Targeted greps found no notion that
duplicates one in the pinned Mathlib. Its left and right variants follow Mathlib's inner ones:
`leftAnodyneExtensions_unionProd_ι` and `rightAnodyneExtensions_unionProd_ι` match
`innerAnodyneExtensions_unionProd_ι`, and all three are proved through Moss pairings.

| Library part | Risk of clashing with upstream work |
|---|---|
| `Equivalence/WeakEquivalence`, `WeakEquivalenceKan` | **high**: Kan–Quillen weak equivalences are Riou's TODO; reconcile when they land |
| `Horn/Spine` (`innerAnodyneExtensions_spineSubcomplex_ι`) | medium: a natural next lemma next to Mathlib's `spine` and strict Segal |
| `Nerve/NerveGroupoidKan`, `Nerve/NerveMap` | medium: small and general |
| `Anodyne/UniversalColimits`, `BaseChangeClosure` | low: general category theory |
| `Fibration/`, `Join/`, `Slice/`, `Cofinal/`, `RelInitial/`, and the left and right files of `Anodyne/` | low: no visible upstream work on this layer |

A subdivision of posets is now in Mathlib (`PartialOrder.NonemptyFiniteChains`,
`Mathlib/Order/NonemptyFiniteChains.lean`, used by `SimplexCategory.sd`). Work on subdivisions
here should build on it.

**`Simplicial/` (2024, Lean `v4.9.0-rc3`)**, about 430 lines, is covered by Mathlib file by

| `SimplexCategory/Generators` | 38 | `SimplexCategory.δ`, `σ` in `SimplexCategory/Basic.lean` |

Where the 2024 library differs:

- It asks fillers of `Λ[n, i]` for every `n`, including `n = 0`, and `Λ[0, 0]` is empty. So, for
  `S : SSet.{0}`, its `KanComplex S` holds if and only if `S` is Kan in Mathlib's sense and
  nonempty. Its `Quasicategory` (horns `Λ[n + 2, i]`, `0 < i < n + 2`) is the standard one.
- It works in universe 0, and its horns and boundaries are separate simplicial sets with an
  inclusion, not subcomplexes.
- It was never ported past `v4.9.0-rc3` and probably does not build against current Mathlib
  (not tried).

## 4. Upstream work in progress

### 4.1 What Mathlib's TODOs announce

These items are marked "TODO @joelriou" in the pinned Mathlib:

- the Kan–Quillen model structure "fully upstreamed", and then anodyne extensions as exactly
  the trivial cofibrations (`SimplicialSet/AnodyneExtensions/Basic.lean`). "Fully upstreamed"

  (`SimplicialSet/Subdivision.lean`);

The TODO list of `AnodyneExtensions/Basic.lean` is partly out of date: the pushout-product of
a horn and a boundary, and the inner variants, are already proved (section 3.1). Docstring TODOs
are therefore weak evidence of what is still to come.

## 5. Reedy model structures: the `reedy` repository

[`joelriou/reedy`](https://github.com/joelriou/reedy) came out of the Mittag-Leffler workshop
*Formalizing higher categories*. It formalizes the Reedy model structure on `C ⥤ D`, following
Riehl–Verity, *Elements of ∞-Category Theory*, sections C.4 and C.5. Its README lists six goals:

1. Reedy categories, with the example `SimplexCategory`;
2. the skeleton filtration of the Yoneda functor as a relative cell complex;
3. latching and matching objects;
4. the skeleton and coskeleton filtration of functors `C ⥤ D`;
5. a weak factorization system on `D` gives one on `C ⥤ D`;
6. the Reedy model structure.

It also needs `Subfunctor` and `Subfunctor₂` API, and weighted limits and colimits.

State of the local clone: the remote `main` was still at commit `5e5cfd8` on 2026-07-19, the date
of the last fast-forward. Its `lakefile.toml` requires the fork `joelriou/mathlib4`, branch
`pushout-obj-obj-refactor`, pinned with Lean `v4.32.0-rc1`. That is older than this repository's
pin, so the table below compares code of different dates. Goals 1 to 4 are written. Goal 5 has a
`sorry` in the factorization (`Reedy/Reedy/WeakFactorizationSystem/Factorization.lean`,
C.5.7). Goal 6 has two `sorry`s in `Reedy/Reedy/ModelCategory.lean` (`trivialCofibrations_eq`,
`trivialFibrations_eq`, C.5.13).

Module names in the table are relative to the source root `Reedy/` (so `Reedy/Basic` is the
file `Reedy/Reedy/Basic.lean`).

| `reedy` module | Counterpart in the pinned Mathlib |
|---|---|
| `Reedy/Basic` | yes, and larger: `AlgebraicTopology/Reedy/Basic.lean` (`HomotopicalAlgebra.ReedyStructure`) |
| `Reedy/SimplexCategory` | not yet |
| `Reedy/RelativeCellComplex`, `Latching`, `Matching`, `Skeleton` | not yet; the framework is in `AlgebraicTopology/RelativeCellComplex/` |
| `Reedy/WeakFactorizationSystem/*`, `Reedy/ModelCategory` | not yet; the general API is in `CategoryTheory/MorphismProperty/WeakFactorizationSystem.lean` |
| `WeightedLimits/Limits` | partly: `CategoryTheory/Limits/Weighted/` (no `weightedLim₂` or adjunction parts) |
| `WeightedLimits/Colimits` | not yet |
| `Subfunctor/SubfunctorTwo`, `ExternalUnionProd`, `Colimits` | not yet; the one-variable `Subfunctor` is in `CategoryTheory/Subfunctor/` |
| `Limits/PushoutObjObjObj`, `TriplePushouts` | partly: `PushoutObjObj` is in `CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/PullbackObjObj.lean`; the triple version is not |

**What may move into Mathlib (inference).** `Reedy/Basic`, the one-variable `Subfunctor`, the
core of weighted limits and two `MorphismProperty` lemmas have counterparts in Mathlib. The one
explicit roadmap signal is the TODO in Mathlib's `Reedy/Basic.lean`, which links
`leanprover-community/project-intentions` issue 5 on the Reedy model structure; that issue was
not read. Likely next: weighted colimits, `Subfunctor₂`, triple pushouts, then latching and
matching objects, the weak factorization system and the model structure.

## 6. Method and limits

- Mathlib was read in the clone at `.lake/packages/mathlib`, revision `110cb7f`. Mathlib
  `master` was at `b63f6e8` when the clone was made (2026-09-27), so the pin is older than
  `master`. Every Mathlib claim comes from reading or grepping this tree.
- "Not found" means targeted greps, with several spellings, found nothing. It is strong
  evidence, not proof.
- Not checked: Joël Riou's pull requests (merged and open), the GitHub history of `reedy`, a
  declaration-by-declaration comparison with Mathlib `master`, and `lake build`. These need
  network access, which the survey session did not have. `reedy` was read in a local clone.
- The draft went through five independent review rounds: Mathlib facts, Riou and `reedy` facts,
  mathematics, structure and scope, and prose.
